## Short description (shown at the top on some layouts)

AutoMoto AI (Beta) is an AI assistant that does the work: it researches, writes Word, Excel and PowerPoint files,
works with your data, and asks before anything risky. Needs your own AI key, a local AI model, or an AutoMoto account.

## Description

```
Requires: an AI service to talk to. Add your own key for an AI provider (free keys are available from NVIDIA NIM, Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter), run a local AI model on your PC (Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp), or sign in to an AutoMoto account server someone runs for you. Local mode also installs the free AutoMoto engine (Python) on first use, after asking you.

BETA: this is a test version. Things may change or break. Please report problems.

AutoMoto AI is an AI assistant that does real work for you, and asks before anything risky.

Say what you need, or attach a file:
- "Make a Word report about the benefits of exercise, with headings and a table of contents"
- "Summarise my slides" (attach a .pptx) or "Analyse my spreadsheet and chart the totals"
- "Research the top 3 tech headlines every morning at 8"

It picks the right tools for the task, shows each step in plain words, and stops for your approval before it writes files, runs a command or contacts another service. You can see and undo what it did.

Your choice of AI: bring your own key, use a free local model that runs with no internet, or sign in to an account to see the same chats and files on every device.

Private by design: no telemetry, no ads. Your chats and files stay on your computer (or on the account server you choose) and only go to the AI provider you picked.

Uses live generative AI: replies and documents are written by the AI model you choose and can be wrong. Check important results. Use "Report a problem" in the app to report harmful or inappropriate output.

Free and open source under the GNU AGPL v3. A commercial licence is available for closed-source or commercial use. Made by Yasin Mohammed Miah.
```
